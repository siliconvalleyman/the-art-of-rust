### EX-C046-01 — Trace a stack and a queue

**Beginner.** Insert `red` and `blue` into an empty stack and into
a queue with two slots. Remove one item from each and give the
remaining order. Verify both with owned `String` values.

### EX-C046-02 — Count work for geometric growth

**Beginner.** With the doubling model, record capacity and number
of moves for each of the first nine pushes. Give the total including
the insertion writes and explain what changes when pushing item ten.

### EX-C046-03 — Verify the full-state contract

**Intermediate.** With capacity one, try to enqueue two records.
Recover the rejected value from the error. After removing the first
record, enqueue the rejected one again and verify its value.

### EX-C046-04 — Bound the wrapped tail index

**Intermediate.** Given `head < capacity` and `offset < capacity`,
implement the two-branch wrapped index. Demonstrate the boundary with
`head = usize::MAX - 1`, `offset = 2`, and `capacity = usize::MAX`.
Prove why using `(head + offset) % capacity` can fail for that input.

### EX-C046-05 — Compare against an independent queue model

**Challenge.** Generate all four-operation sequences, choosing at
each step between pushing its step number and popping. Compare
capacity two against an independent
`VecDeque` model. Include checks for returned values, behavior on
rejection, and final order, and state what your coverage leaves open.
