### EX-C029-01 — Give the units distinct types

**Beginner.** Model whole centimeters and whole
millimeters as different wrappers. Convert centimeters to
millimeters without overflowing a `u16` representation.
Test zero, a normal value, and the first input whose
converted value does not fit.

### EX-C029-02 — Reject a fractional conversion

**Intermediate.** Convert milligrams to whole grams
only when the input is exactly divisible by 1,000.
Keep distinct input and output wrappers. Compare
4,000 and 4,500 mg. Explain why straightforward integer
division fails the stricter contract.

### EX-C029-03 — Derive a constructor contract

**Intermediate.** Represent a shelf position chosen
from 1 through 24. Give the type a private field and
an optional constructor. Test zero, both accepted
endpoints, and 25. Prove that every successfully
constructed position satisfies the predicate.

### EX-C029-04 — Add at the policy boundary

**Intermediate.** Start with a parcel of weight 29,999 g.
Check adding zero, one, two, and the maximum unsigned
32-bit value. Explain which accepted and rejected
arithmetic paths those tests exercise.

### EX-C029-05 — Audit all mutation paths

**Challenge.** A valid volume wrapper admits 1 through
100. It exposes a checked constructor, an immutable
observer, and a method returning `&mut u16`.
Show which method breaks the promise and provide a safe
replacement returning a result after validation.
Check that a rejected update leaves the old value usable.

- **IQ-C029-02, intermediate.** Why is a checked
