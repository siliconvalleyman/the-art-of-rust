### EX-C038-01 — Trace exhaustion

**Beginner.** Create a countdown for two and record
the outputs of its first four calls. Include the remaining
count before and after every call.

### EX-C038-02 — Check the exact size hint

**Beginner.** Test a countdown for three before taking
an item, after taking one, and after exhaustion. Explain
why neither bound should change once the state is zero.

### EX-C038-03 — Check lazy mapping

**Intermediate.** Map a countdown's values with a
callback that also records a call count. Create the
adapter, take one item, and verify that the callback
runs once. Explain what is deferred and what has already
been created.

### EX-C038-04 — Implement an ascending byte iterator

**Intermediate.** Implement a stateful iterator yielding
0 through `n−1` for an initial `u8` bound `n`.
Handle zero and the maximum bound. Explain why the
increment fits during every successful call.

### EX-C038-05 — Compare absence contracts

**Challenge.** Construct an iterator that yields 7,
then None, then 9 on successive calls. Wrap it with
`fuse` and compare the third result. Explain why the
wrapper is relevant even if one consumer stops at the
first absence.
