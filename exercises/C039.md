### EX-C039-01 — Observe adapter order

**Beginner.** Given `[1, 3, 4]`, compare adding
one before filtering for even values against filtering
for even inputs before adding one. Test both outputs.

### EX-C039-02 — Supply a fold identity

**Beginner.** Sum a slice of bytes with a `u32`
accumulator. Use checked addition and give the
result for an empty slice and for `[255, 1]`.
Explain the role of the initial value.

### EX-C039-03 — Preserve empty input in a reduction

**Intermediate.** Implement the minimum byte with
`reduce`. Distinguish an empty slice from a slice
containing only the greatest byte. Derive the
nonempty-prefix invariant.

### EX-C039-04 — Find the first parse or sum failure

**Intermediate.** Prepare one input that overflows
before reaching a malformed third field and one
that fails to parse its second field. Test the
reported indices and errors.

### EX-C039-05 — Demonstrate unconsumed suffix work

**Challenge.** Use `try_fold` on a mutable iterator
over three fields. Fail at the second field. Verify
that a subsequent `next` on the iterator returns
the third and explain which items the reduction
actually obtained.

### Knowledge check

- **IQ-C039-01, beginner.** What different empty-input
  behavior does reduce offer compared with fold?
- **IQ-C039-02, intermediate.** How can reordering
  parsing and a fallible aggregation change which
  error is reported?
